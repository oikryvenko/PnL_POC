02End-to-end architecture

Each layer has a distinct purpose: capture source data, establish validated business records, and publish reporting-ready outputs.

03Databricks workflow

Six Python-wheel tasks run as a dependency-aware Databricks Job. Prices and trades can be ingested and validated independently; Gold calculations wait for both validated inputs.

Deployment

Python package deployed with Databricks Asset Bundles. Weekday schedule is configured but paused in the development environment.

04Data quality & trade lifecycle

Silver trades

Type checks and business-rule validation are applied before trades are made available for valuation. Records failing validation are retained with rejection reasons.

Silver market prices

Closing prices are validated and made available by symbol and trading date. Invalid prices are retained in a separate rejection table.

Trade correction policy

For each trade_id, the highest version is authoritative; modified_timestamp resolves ordering. If that latest version is invalid, an older version is not silently substituted.

Business-key matching and Delta MERGE operations support repeatable processing and reduce duplicate outputs on reruns.

05Positions & daily P&amp;L

Positions are built from signed trade quantities: buys increase holdings; sells reduce them. Daily P&L is then calculated per valuation date, book and instrument using closing prices.

Daily P&LOpening quantity × (Today close − Previous close)
+ Σ [Signed trade quantity × (Today close − Execution price)]

Existing positions

The first term captures the market-price movement on holdings carried into the session.

Today’s trades

The second term captures the mark-to-close contribution of trades executed during the session.

Missing data is visible

Where a required price is unavailable, the pipeline preserves the missing valuation rather than presenting it as zero, and records a valuation break for investigation.

08Design choices & next steps

This implementation is a proof of concept, intentionally scoped to demonstrate a complete and understandable trading valuation workflow.

Current design choices

Yahoo Finance supplies equity prices; the trade input is synthetic; some processing recalculates full history to keep correction handling straightforward.

Production evolution

Integrate an approved enterprise price feed, expand exchange-calendar coverage, introduce incremental recalculation, and strengthen automated deployment and operational controls.

Provider choice and paused development scheduling are explicit scope decisions, not claims of production readiness.

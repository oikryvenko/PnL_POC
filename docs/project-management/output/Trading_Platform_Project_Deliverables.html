Slide 7

Position and P&L calculation design

06 | FINANCIAL LOGIC

TRADING PLATFORM | EVIDENCE BASELINE 2026-10-14 | PROPOSED DELIVERY PLAN

07

Position calculation | implemented

BUY quantity is positive; SELL quantity is negative. Daily net = SUM(signed trade quantity) Closing position = cumulative SUM(daily net) Market value = closing position × same-day close Grain: date / book / counterparty / instrument / currency. No-trade sessions carry the position. Price gaps leave value NULL and create a break.

Daily P&L | simplified, implemented

Opening quantity = closing quantity − daily net quantity Daily P&L = OpeningQty × (CurrentClose − PreviousClose) + (DailyNetQty × CurrentClose − signed trade consideration) The trade-day term captures execution-to-close economics without applying the full day's price move to new quantity. Missing current close, or missing prior baseline for nonzero opening inventory, yields NULL + break.

Example: opening 10 shares; buy 5 at 106; prior close 105; current close 108. Price move = 10×3 = 30; trade-day term = 5×108−5×106 = 10; daily P&L = 40. Cash, fees, realized/unrealized accounting, FX, corporate actions, and accounting sign-off are outside this prototype.

Speaker notes: TPM perspective: state assumptions before results. Use reconciliation and Product Control acceptance as gates; do not generalize the prototype formula to all instruments.
